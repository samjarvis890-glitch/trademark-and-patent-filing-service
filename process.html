OUR PROCESS

A Clear Path From Idea To Filing

Trademark and patent filing can feel complex when the steps are unclear. Our process is designed to organize the journey, prepare the right information, review the details carefully, and move the application forward with greater clarity.

UNDERSTAND → PREPARE → REVIEW → REFINE → FILE → FOLLOW THROUGH

EVERY STEP HAS A PURPOSE.

From the first conversation to the stages that follow filing, each part of the process is focused on keeping information organized and expectations clear.

The exact requirements and procedural steps may vary depending on the type of application and applicable filing requirements.

Good Filings Begin With Good Questions.

Before documents are prepared, we first need to understand what you are trying to protect, what you already have, and what you want the filing process to accomplish.

01 — UNDERSTAND

WHAT TO PROTECT

What are you trying to protect? We begin by understanding your brand, invention, business context, and the purpose behind the application.

02 — COLLECT

AVAILABLE ASSETS

What information is available? Relevant names, descriptions, drawings, ownership information, business details, and supporting materials are identified and organized.

03 — DEFINE

NEXT PRIORITIES

What needs to happen next? The available information is used to establish a clear starting point for preparation and review.

The Process, Step By Step.

Every filing follows a sequence of preparation, review, submission, and follow-through. The six stages below provide a simple view of how that journey is organized.

01 INITIATION

DISCOVER

Understand the brand, invention, objective, and information available at the starting point.

02 ASSEMBLY

PREPARE

Organize the documents, descriptions, drawings, and other information needed for the application.

03 ANALYSIS

REVIEW

Examine the prepared information for consistency, clarity, completeness, and potential gaps.

04 OPTIMIZATION

REFINE

Address questions, clarify information, and make the application materials ready for filing.

05 SUBMISSION

FILE

Move the completed application through the appropriate filing route and submission process.

06 MONITORING

FOLLOW THROUGH

Keep the next procedural steps organized after filing and understand what happens from there.

What Happens Along The Way?

The process becomes easier to understand when each stage has a clear purpose. Explore what each step is designed to accomplish.

The process begins by understanding what you want to protect, the nature of the brand or invention, and the outcome you are working toward.

Relevant information and supporting materials are collected and organized for the application and its requirements.

The prepared information is reviewed for consistency, clarity, completeness, and areas that may need further attention.

Questions and information gaps are addressed so the application materials can be brought into a more complete state.

Once the application materials are prepared and ready, the filing is submitted through the appropriate filing route.

After submission, the applicable procedural stages and next steps are kept organized so the process remains clear.

Prepared. Reviewed. Filed.

Before an application moves forward, the details matter. Review and preparation help create a more organized filing, while follow-through keeps the next stages visible after submission.
