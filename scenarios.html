SCENARIOS

WHEN THE BUSINESS CHANGES, PROTECTION SHOULD KEEP UP.

Trademark and patent needs often appear at important moments — when a new brand is taking shape, an invention is becoming real, a business is expanding, or an idea is ready to move into the market.

DIFFERENT MOMENTS. DIFFERENT QUESTIONS. ONE CLEARER WAY FORWARD.

Explore the situations below to see where trademark and patent filing support may fit into your journey.

SCENARIO 01

YOU HAVE A NAME. NOW YOU NEED TO KNOW WHAT COMES NEXT.

A business name can become one of the most recognizable parts of a growing business. Before investing heavily in identity, packaging, marketing, or expansion, it can be important to understand how the name fits into your protection strategy.

01

NEW BRAND

You are preparing to launch a business, product, or service and want to understand the protection considerations around the name you plan to use.

CONSIDER

The name, the intended use, and the information available before moving forward.

02

GROWING IDENTITY

Your business is gaining recognition and the name or identity has become increasingly important to your future plans.

CONSIDER

Whether the existing brand information and protection strategy still match your direction.

03

NEW DIRECTION

You are introducing a new product, service, brand name, or market-facing identity and need to understand the next step.

CONSIDER

What is changing, what needs attention, and what should be prepared before filing.

SCENARIO 02

THE IDEA IS MOVING FROM CONCEPT TO CREATION.

When a product, technical concept, or invention begins taking shape, questions about documentation, development, ownership, and protection can become increasingly important.

01

CONCEPT

The idea begins to take shape through sketches, descriptions, prototypes, or early technical thinking.

02

DEVELOPMENT

The concept develops through testing, refinement, technical work, or practical product development.

03

DOCUMENTATION

Relevant technical information, drawings, descriptions, and development details are brought together.

04

PROTECTION

The available information can then be considered in relation to the appropriate protection and filing pathway.

WHEN THE BUSINESS GROWS, THE QUESTIONS CAN CHANGE.

A growing business may introduce new products, enter new markets, develop new identities, or build additional intellectual property. Growth can create new questions around what already exists and what comes next.

01

NEW MARKET

Your business is preparing to move into a different geographic or commercial market.

WATCH FOR

Changes in the way the brand, product, or invention will be used.

02

NEW PRODUCT

An established business is developing a new product or offering that introduces new intellectual property.

WATCH FOR

New names, product identities, technical developments, or supporting materials.

03

BRAND EXPANSION

Your existing identity is being extended across additional products, services, or business activities.

WATCH FOR

Whether the existing protection strategy still reflects the expanded direction.

04

BUSINESS DEVELOPMENT

Your business is becoming more established and intellectual property is becoming more central to future plans.

WATCH FOR

Important assets, ownership information, and decisions that may affect future growth.
